Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Regime-Adaptive Risk-Aware MPPI Control for
Force-Aware Robotic Wiping with Temporal-Difference World Models” for
publication in the IEEE Open Journal of the Computer Society.

The article presents a computation-aware extension of TD-MPC2 for contact-rich
robotic wiping. A force-conditioned world model drives a regime-adaptive,
risk-aware MPPI planner that changes both its planning objective and sampling
budget from causal contact evidence. Across 270 paired evaluations, the method
raises compound pass from 85/135 to 100/135 while using eight fewer planning
samples and 25.7 ms less median planning time. A separate zero-shot MuJoCo
study achieves compound pass in all 30 flat and inclined trials and identifies curved
contact as the remaining portability boundary.

The manuscript focuses on world-model control, adaptive computation, rigorous
simulation evaluation, and reproducible software artifacts. It is original,
has not been published previously, and is not under review elsewhere. The
author declares no competing interests and will provide public code and an
archival data record.

Thank you for your consideration.
