\section{Artifact Package}
\label{app:artifact_package}

The public reproducibility package contains task and factor-block definitions,
training and evaluation scripts, compact frozen results, bootstrap analyses,
cross-simulator calibration records, numerical-sensitivity cells, and
verification tests. Five checkpoints and the step-level evaluation traces are
stored in a separate versioned archival record. Repository and archive
manifests record the relative path, byte count, and SHA-256 digest of every
payload file. Reproduction starts from the environment record and checkpoint
manifest, then runs the frozen evaluators and independent analyses against the
recorded trace keys.

\section{Direct PPO Configuration}
\label{app:ppo_configuration}

The direct PPO reference uses a 16-dimensional observation, a
three-dimensional Cartesian action, two hidden layers of width 256, a
learning rate of $3\times10^{-4}$, discount 0.99, generalized-advantage
parameter 0.95, clip ratio 0.2, entropy coefficient 0.005, and value
coefficient 0.5. Each update uses 2,048 rollout transitions, ten epochs, and
eight minibatches per epoch, with gradient norm clipped at 0.5.

The budget study checkpoints the default setting at 82,774, 165,548, and
331,096 transitions. Six one-factor variants use 165,548 transitions and
change learning rate to $10^{-4}$ or $10^{-3}$, clip ratio to 0.1 or 0.3, or
entropy coefficient to 0 or 0.01. Table~\ref{tab:ppo_dev_sensitivity} reports
all development endpoints. Every endpoint contains 90 evaluations; authority
passes are 90/90 throughout. The fixed selection rule chose clip ratio 0.1 at
2$\times$ budget. No final-suite result was used for selection.

\section{Cell-Level Numerical-Sensitivity Results}
\label{app:numerical_cells}

Tables~\ref{tab:numerical_cells_f1_f2} and
\ref{tab:numerical_cells_f4_f5} report every factor--sentinel cell used in the
numerical-sensitivity analysis. The three-bit suffix records task, tracking,
and sampled-force-limit pass status in that order.

The mesh factory received all 12 requested source resolutions. Ten cooked
payloads matched the 48-segment baseline digest; the eight-segment F4 request
retained the same 24-triangle connectivity, while its 14 vertex coordinates
differed by at most $1.39\times10^{-17}$~m. Only the 96-segment F4 request
changed the topology and triggered the source-resolution peak rule.

\section{Cross-Simulator Calibration Gate}
\label{app:crosssim_gate}

The MuJoCo port was calibrated without loading a policy checkpoint. Three
contact-free motions identified a two-sample delay and 80-ms first-order
actuator response. Four exposed contact motions then selected the contact and
drive parameters. Table~\ref{tab:crosssim_validation} gives the errors on two
new held-out motions. The multilevel normal cycle meets every tolerance. The
bidirectional tangential motion meets the force, onset, and peak tolerances,
but its 0.833-mm position RMSE exceeds the 0.5-mm limit. The later policy run
is therefore a zero-shot stress test rather than matched-engine replication.
