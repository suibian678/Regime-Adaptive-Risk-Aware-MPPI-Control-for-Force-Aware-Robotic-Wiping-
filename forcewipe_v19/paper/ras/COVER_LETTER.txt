Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Regime-Adaptive Risk-Aware MPPI Control for
Force-Aware Robotic Wiping with Temporal-Difference World Models” for
publication as a Research Article in Robotics and Autonomous Systems.

The manuscript develops a force-conditioned TD-MPC2 controller with
regime-adaptive risk-aware model-predictive path integral planning for
contact-rich robotic wiping. Its central contribution is a causal adaptation
of planning cost and computational budget across contact regimes. The method
is evaluated through paired factor-separated simulation, matched direct PPO
and adaptive-admittance references, numerical-sensitivity analysis,
deployment-gap stress tests, and zero-shot transfer of five frozen checkpoints
to a MuJoCo task port.

Across 270 paired evaluations, the proposed planner raises compound pass from
85/135 to 100/135 and reduces median planning time by 25.7 ms. Cross-engine
evaluation retains all 30 flat and inclined compound outcomes and identifies
weakly curved contact as the remaining portability boundary. The paper reports
this as simulation robustness rather than hardware validation, and it separates
task-level evidence from setting-sensitive force-tail statistics.

This work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not been
published previously. The author has approved the manuscript and declares no
competing interests. Code, protocols, compact results, checkpoints, and traces
are being prepared for public archival release.

Thank you for your consideration.
